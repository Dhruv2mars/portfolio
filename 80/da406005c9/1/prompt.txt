You like a specific taste culture — not “nice portfolios” in general. Across all seven, the shared DNA is quiet confidence: the site proves skill by restraint, not by volume.

What’s common
1. The person is the product
None of these open like a generic agency site. It’s “I’m Eric,” “hola i’m pabs,” “Evil Rabbit is a designer…,” “john phamous.” Name, voice, and presence sit above role titles.

2. Extreme restraint
Especially Pablo, Pham, Logan, Evil Rabbit: narrow column, black/white, almost no chrome. The flex is “I don’t need a hero video.” Even the more visual ones (Eftakher, Eric, Dai) still feel edited, not stuffed.

3. Monochrome + type does the work
Near-black or near-white. Serif/sans mixes, or mono. Hierarchy from weight and spacing — not cards, gradients, or icon rows.

4. Design × engineering identity
Design engineers, “I ship in code,” components, systems, AI tools, Vercel orbit. You’re drawn to builders who make the site itself the proof, not just screenshots of Figma files.

5. Alive, current signals
Live clocks, location, “right now,” availability status, contribution graphs, “today / latest.” They feel like people who are currently shipping — not a frozen résumé from 2021.

6. Craft in the small things
Pill navs, hover underlines, theme toggles, ⌘K, name pronunciation, staggered fades, custom selection colors, playful easter eggs (Mello, obfuscated project names). Taste shows up in details, not decoration.

7. Pedigree stated calmly
Vercel, Auth0, NASA, acquisitions, angel checks — listed like facts, not trophies. Soft authority.

8. Personality without performing
Lowercase, dry humor, hobbies, side projects, essays. Human, but never try-hard or “fun startup” loud.

9. Systems over case-study theater
Components, design systems, creative tools, brand systems, AI products. Work about how things get made, not just “I redesigned a landing page.”

Why you like them so much
You’re responding to authored taste — sites that feel like one person’s hand, not a template.

You want the feeling of: this person is so good they can afford to be quiet. That quietness reads as status in the design-engineer world.

There’s a soft split in your list, but both sides feed the same craving:

Cluster	Sites	What hits you
Visual craft
Eftakher, Eric Sin, Dai
Grids, polish, beautiful work presentation, still restrained
Text as flex
Pablo, Pham, Logan, Evil Rabbit
Ultra-minimal CVs where confidence = subtraction
What you’re really attracted to isn’t “dark mode” or “minimalism” as trends. It’s:

Clarity over salesiness
Identity over portfolio theater
Craft over decoration
Builder energy over pure visual designer energy
Calm ego — strong presence without shouting
In one line: you like portfolios that feel like a signature, not a brochure.
